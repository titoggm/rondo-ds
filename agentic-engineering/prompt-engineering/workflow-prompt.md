# Workflow Prompt

> Squential prompt with input, work, and output.

- Sections

  - Instructions. (secondary)
  - Variables (secondary)
  - Report (secondary)
  - Relevant Files

The workflow is the exact step by step play of exactly what you want your agent to do.
Instructions are surrounding information on the individual step in the workflow
